Why Open-Source AI Matters

1. Zero Cloud Egress (100% Privacy)

Cloud SaaS (QuickBooks, Expensify, OpenAI) transfers your bank slips and vendor info to remote servers. LedgerVault runs on-device via Tesseract WASM and Ollama: zero data ever leaves your device.

2. $0.00 Inference Overhead

Traditional SaaS burns cloud API credits on every single scan. LedgerVault leverages client CPU/WASM: zero inference costs allow a sustainable $29 lifetime model with ~96% gross margins.

3. 100% Offline Resilience

Works anywhere without internet connectivity—on flights, trains, or job sites. All receipts and vector data remain encrypted in your local browser IndexedDB.

4. Model Independence

Zero vendor lock-in. Switch seamlessly between in-browser WASM, local Ollama (Llama 3.2, Qwen 2.5), or lightweight deterministic parsers.
